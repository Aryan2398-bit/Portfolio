FAQs

What does your design process look like?
I start with research — understanding the user, the business context, and the technical constraints. From there I move through information architecture, low-fidelity exploration, and then high-fidelity prototypes. I work closely with engineers throughout, not just at handoff.
Are you available for freelance projects?
Yes, I take select freelance engagements — particularly in fintech, banking, or financial services. I'm most useful for 0→1 product work, redesigns, or design system creation. Get in touch to discuss scope.
What industries do you specialise in?
My primary domain is fintech — payments, wealth management, lending, and neobanking. I have deep familiarity with regulatory constraints, trust signals, and the specific UX challenges that financial products face.
Do you work with early-stage startups?
Yes. I enjoy 0→1 work and have shipped products from concept to launch. I'm comfortable with ambiguity and can help define the product as much as design it.
Can you work with an existing design system?
Absolutely. I've worked with both in-house and third-party design systems. I'm also experienced in building and maintaining design systems from scratch, including component libraries and documentation.
